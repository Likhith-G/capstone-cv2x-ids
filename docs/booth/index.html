Four moves

In order. Each one only makes sense after the one before it.

  1. Lie with one receiver listeningGo as far as you like. Nothing happens. One receiver does not do this badly, it cannot do it at all.
  2. Switch the other receivers onNow there is a boundary. Find the smallest lie you still get away with and you have found the detection floor yourself.
  3. Lie sideways instead of along the roadYou get away with much more. The receivers sit in a line, so across the road they can barely see.
  4. Turn on the map constraintThe detector now knows a car cannot be off the carriageway. The sideways trick stops working.
Every number on this page was computed by analysis/make_booth_surface.py over the real dataset, using the same consistency statistic the paper reports. The page is a lookup over that measurement sampled on a grid, not an illustration of it. The threshold is the 95th percentile of the ratio over honest stations, so caught means the claim looks worse than 95 in 100 honest cars.
