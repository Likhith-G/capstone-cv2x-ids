The careful liar

One receiver can't tell a lie from a fade.

A car that shifts every claim by the same amount stays self-consistent. Content checks score zero on it.

The main result

Many receivers, pooled, can see it.

A false position is shared by every receiver. Fading is not. Pool the measurements, and the lie shows above a floor.

m

The smart attacker

Lie sideways, and the map catches you.

Receivers sit along the road, so position across it is measured worst. A map check closes exactly that gap.

When the road changes

New conditions, no new labels.

Left unchanged, the detector fails mostly by false alarms. Refitting its input scaling on unlabelled local traffic recovered most of the loss in two of three changes. Each card is a separate test.
